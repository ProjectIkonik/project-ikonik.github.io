04 // TRAINING PROGRAM

HOW TO PLAY A MATCH

Select a stage to read general Battle Royale tips.

START WITH A SMART DROP

  1. Check the Battle Bus route before jumping.
  2. Choose a landing spot with buildings and loot.
  3. For a quieter start, avoid the busiest boss hideouts.
  4. Look around while gliding to see where other players land.

BUILD A BALANCED INVENTORY

  1. Find close-range and medium-range weapons early.
  2. Collect shields and healing items.
  3. Keep ammunition and materials stocked where applicable.
  4. Upgrade weaker equipment when you find better loot.

STAY AHEAD OF THE STORM

  1. Check the next safe zone as soon as it appears.
  2. Start moving before the storm reaches you.
  3. Use hills, trees, and buildings as cover.
  4. Watch for opponents rotating from nearby locations.

TAKE BETTER FIGHTS

  1. Use cover instead of standing exposed.
  2. Choose a weapon suited to the distance.
  3. Reload and heal when you have a safe opportunity.
  4. Know when to disengage instead of chasing every opponent.

PLAY THE FINAL CIRCLES

  1. Keep track of the shrinking safe zone.
  2. Save healing supplies for when you need them.
  3. Prioritize good positioning.
  4. Stay patient and listen for nearby opponents.
